# CannActuality — Brief Topic Queue

The weekly research run takes the top **Open** topic, researches it with web
search (opening every source it cites), writes the brief, publishes it with
`tools/new_brief.py`, and moves the topic to **Done** with its date and slug.

Rules for every brief: every number traces to an opened, cited source
(publisher + date); no health or benefit claims; no dosing or consumption
advice; a "What this means for product teams" section; a "research, not legal
or medical advice" note; publication date on the page.

## Open

1. **The December 11 readiness brief: converting hemp SKUs to licensed channels** — A state-by-state checklist (CA first) for brands migrating intoxicating hemp products into licensed cannabis channels before the federal deadline.
2. **Vape category teardown: hardware, flavor systems, and the disposables question** — Vapor pens are California's #2 category; map format and flavor trends so hardware and oil decisions match what is actually selling.
3. **Edibles after the beverage boom: gummies' share, fast-acting formats, and price ladders** — Edibles declined 3.8% in CA's April 2026 data; find where edible development money still earns a return.
4. **Medical-channel economics after Schedule III: what the DEA registration pathway changes for product teams** — The April 2026 final order created federal registration for state medical licensees; translate it into portfolio and packaging implications (with the 280E caveat flagged, not assumed).
5. **New-state market entry 2026–2027: reading maturity before capital** — A framework brief scoring emerging adult-use states on category mix, price compression, and retail density so entrants size their launch portfolio correctly.
6. **Flavor as a development discipline: what the 2026 flavor data says across vapes, gummies, and beverages** — Flavor is the top stated purchase driver in vapes and the fastest-moving beverage variable; consolidate the evidence into formulation guidance.
7. **Retail buyer brief: assortment planning for a flat market** — For dispensary buyers: category roles, price-ladder evidence, and beverage/pre-roll allocation when total sales are declining at high single digits.
8. **Regulatory watch update: rescheduling after the stay, FDA's cannabinoid list, and state hemp laws** — Refresh the October 2026 regulatory brief with whatever the ALJ, FDA, and state legislatures have done since; re-date and re-verify every status.
9. **Concentrates' niche: who is still buying, and what premium means there** — Concentrates fell 9.6% in CA April 2026 data but remain a high-value enthusiast category; map the surviving segments for targeted, smaller-run product development.
10. **Packaging and claims compliance as a design input, 2026** — Child-resistance, labeling, and advertising restrictions by major state, framed as design constraints to brief packaging teams with — not legal advice.
11. **Cultivar and chemotype stability: genetics as a product spec** — How much of finished-product variance is genetic vs environmental; what seed/clone sourcing and chemotype verification mean for brands buying biomass.
12. **Irrigation and substrate systems: coco, rockwool, living soil** — Compare the controlled evidence on yield, consistency, cost, and failure modes across the three dominant substrate strategies.
13. **Integrated pest management and microbial control for licensed cultivation** — Predatory controls, UV-C, and sanitation protocols vs the state microbial testing limits they have to pass; what the evidence supports at commercial scale.
14. **Extraction inputs: how biomass quality decisions price into oil** — Fresh-frozen vs cured material, storage before extraction, and grade segregation — the upstream choices that determine extract cost and quality.
