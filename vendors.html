Custom briefs

How to request a custom brief

  • 1. Send the decision, not just the topic. “Should we launch a 10 mg seltzer line in California before December?” scopes better than “beverages.”
  • 2. We confirm scope and sources. You get the questions the brief will answer, the data sources it will draw on, and the delivery date — before work starts.
  • 3. You receive a dated, sourced brief in the same format as the public research: takeaways first, evidence second, product implications last.
  • 4. Follow-up is part of the work. One working session to pressure-test the implications against your roadmap is included in every custom engagement.

Custom research is performed for licensed vendors and verified industry professionals. We do not provide legal or compliance sign-off — briefs flag the regulatory questions your counsel should confirm.
