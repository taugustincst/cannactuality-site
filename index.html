What the research covers

Four lenses on every product decision

A product roadmap fails when any one of these moves and nobody notices. CannActuality tracks all four, continuously, and publishes what changes.

Consumers & categories

Demand signals

Category sales, unit velocity, price movement, and format shifts from tracked retail data — which categories are growing, which are consolidating, and where consumers are trading down or trading up.

Formats & formulation

Product intelligence

Deep dives on the formats where development money is moving: beverages, pre-rolls, edibles, vapes, and emerging cannabinoid profiles — strengths, pack architecture, flavor systems, and channel fit.

Channels

Where products win

Dispensary, liquor, convenience, and grocery channels behave differently. Our briefs separate them, because a product tuned for one shelf routinely underperforms on another.

Regulation

Regulatory watch

Federal and state rule changes — rescheduling, hemp redefinition, state integration laws, tax rates — translated into dated product decisions: reformulate, reposition, re-channel, or wait.
