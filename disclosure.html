Disclosure

How CannActuality makes money — and what never influences a brief

Research first

CannActuality publishes market research and education for licensed cannabis vendors and product teams. Every number in every brief is traced to a named, dated source, and the briefs are written before any commercial consideration enters the picture. No company can pay to be covered, to be ranked, or to have a finding softened, and no advertiser or partner sees a brief before it publishes.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site — in briefs, on resource pages, and elsewhere — are affiliate links. If you click one and then buy or sign up with that company, CannActuality may earn a commission or referral fee from the company, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored" and are identified in context where they appear.

Affiliate relationships never determine what the research says. Briefs cite the data sources the industry actually runs on — including companies we have no commercial relationship with — and a product earns a mention by being relevant to the product decision a brief is about, not by having an affiliate program.

Custom research

Vendors can commission custom briefs, category teardowns, and regulatory analysis, described on For Vendors. Commissioned work is scoped and labeled as client work product; it is not published as independent research unless it is released as a public brief under the same sourcing rules as everything else here.

What we are not

CannActuality does not sell, manufacture, or distribute cannabis or hemp products. Nothing on this site is medical, health, or legal advice. Content is intended for adults 21 and over, and cannabis remains regulated at the federal and state level — verify current law in your jurisdiction before making product decisions.
